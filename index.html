Three numbers decide every hit

Each shot runs through the same three checks in order: can it get through the armour (AP vs AV), how much of the damage "sticks" to that body part (raw vs durable damage), and where the damage ends up (the part's own health, the enemy's Main health, or both).

1 · Armour penetration (AP) vs armour value (AV)

Every weapon has an AP level from 0 to 10. Every enemy body part has an AV from 0 to 10. The game compares the two numbers on each hit:

ComparisonResultHitmarker
AP > AV100% damagered
AP = AV65% damagewhite
AP < AV0 damage, the round ricochetsblue / ricochet
Analogy Think of AV as a door's lock grade and AP as the grade of your key. A key one grade higher opens the door fully. A key of the same grade jams halfway, so only about two-thirds gets through. A lower-grade key doesn't turn at all, no matter how hard you push. That is why a 2,000-damage Quasar shot does full damage to a Charger's head (AP6 vs AV4) while 45 Coyote bullets do nothing to the same head (AP3 vs AV4).

Angle matters too: each projectile has separate AP values for straight, slight, large and extreme angles. Most weapons keep their AP until the extreme angle (81–90°), where it usually drops to 0. Explosions lose 1 AP in their outer radius.

2 · Raw damage vs durable damage

Every weapon lists two numbers: standard ("raw") damage and durable damage. Every body part has a durability % between 0 and 100. The part takes a blend of the two, weighted by its durability:

damage to part = raw × (1 − durable%) + durable_dmg × durable% → round down
Analogy A knife and a sledgehammer. Against a curtain (0% durable) the knife is devastating. Against a thick rubber tyre (100% durable) the knife barely marks it and only the hammer's weight counts. Rifle bullets are knives: the Coyote does 75 raw but only 10 durable. Rockets are hammers: an EAT-17 does 2,000 raw and 2,000 durable, so durability doesn't bother it at all.

Worked example: Coyote into a Charger's butt (AV0, 80% durable):
75 × 0.20 + 10 × 0.80 = 15 + 8 = 23 per bullet. AP3 > AV0, so all 23 lands. The butt has 950 HP, so it takes 42 bullets to pop it. Compare that with the bullet's 75 on paper: the durability cut it by about 70%.

Explosions ignore durability. They always deal their durable number (which for every explosion in this sheet equals raw), but each part has an ExDR (explosive damage resistance) that cuts them: a part with 100% ExDR takes no blast damage at all.

3 · Where the damage goes: parts, Main health and fatal parts

Enemies are built from parts. Each part has its own HP and passes a share of every hit (% to Main) into the enemy's Main health pool. The enemy dies when either Main reaches 0, or a fatal part (head, heatsink, butt, belly…) is destroyed.

  • A non-fatal armour plate can only pass its own HP × % into Main. Once it breaks, it's gone, so shooting a Charger's leg armour strips the armour but won't kill it by itself.
  • Some parts have no HP of their own ("Main" in the tables). They feed straight into Main, sometimes at more than 100%: a Charger's inner flesh passes 300%, which is why breaking its side armour and shooting the flesh is so effective.
  • Constitution is a bleed-out buffer. A Charger whose butt is destroyed goes down and bleeds out over a few seconds rather than dying instantly.
Consultant lens It's a gating analysis. AP vs AV is the go / no-go gate (can this option address the problem at all?). Durability is the conversion rate (how much of the input becomes output). Fatal parts and % to Main are the critical path (which workstream actually ends the project). The kill matrix is the recommendation slide; the breakdown tabs are the appendix that backs it.

4 · Fire: the special case

  • Flame weapons fire a stream of particles (2 damage each, 3 for the Cremator) at AP4. Listed DPS: 150/s for the Flamethrower, Torcher, Crisper and Hot Dog; 225/s for the Cremator.
  • Burning adds 100 fire damage per second for 3 s, also AP4, applied only to Main. Against AV4 Main (Chargers, Titans, Hulks) that burn does 65%; against AV5 Main (Vox Engine, Hive Lord) it does nothing.
  • Fire scales with enemy size: Charger / Hulk take 1.5×, Bile Titan / Impaler / Dragonroach 1.7×.
  • The Enhanced Combustion ship module adds +25% fire damage to stratagem fire: the Flamethrower, Cremator, Hot Dog and EAT-700 napalm. It doesn't boost the Torcher, Crisper or Coyote.
flame on part/s = DPS × size mult × (1.25 if stratagem) × AP factor  ·  burn on Main/s = 100 × size mult × (1.25 if stratagem) × AP factor vs Main AV

Flame rows in the breakdown tabs are measured in seconds of continuous spray, not hits.

How to read the breakdown tabs

  1. Pick an enemy and a weapon from the filters.
  2. Each row is one body part. Dmg / hit is after AP and durability. The Math column shows the full calculation.
  3. To break is how many hits (or seconds) destroy that part. To kill is how many end the enemy, and Route says how (fatal part destroyed, or Main drained).
  4. "Breaks part only" means that part is an armour gate: break it, then shoot what's underneath.

Assumptions and limits

  • Ship modules: all unlocked. The only one that directly alters weapon damage values is Enhanced Combustion (+25% fire damage from stratagems). Others affect reload speed, call-in timers, and cooldowns.
  • Range: calculated at point-blank range. Ballistic bullets and plasma bolts suffer damage and AP falloff over long distances; guided missiles (Spear, Commando) do not.
  • Explosions: counted on the primary target part. Real in-game explosions also splash onto adjacent anatomical parts and the Main health pool, meaning area weapons like the Leveller, Ultimatum, and Recoilless HE frequently achieve faster kills than shown in single-part math.
  • Transfer cap: non-fatal armor plates pass at most their own `HP × (% to Main)` into the Main health pool before breaking off.
  • Fire size scaling: direct fire damage scales with target body volume (Hulk/Charger 1.5×, Titan/Impaler 1.7×). Non-stratagem fire weapons (Torcher, Crisper) do not receive the Enhanced Combustion 25% bonus.
  • Purifier: shown as both full charge (1s hold) with AP3 bolt + 300 blast, and quick trigger tap.
  • Coyote fire: AR-2 Coyote is shown both standard and with an assumed 3-second fire burn on Main health.
  • Illuminates (The Squ'ith): energy shields possess AV0 and absorb kinetic energy up to their barrier threshold before breaking and exposing physical anatomy underneath.
  • Patches: Arrowhead frequently adjusts balancing. Stats reflect values verified on helldivers.wiki.gg as of patch 1.007.1xx.
